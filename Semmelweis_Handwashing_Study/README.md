## Overview

This project analyzes historical data from the Vienna General Hospital to investigate the relationship between **handwashing and maternal mortality**.

The analysis is based on data from two clinics between 1841 and 1846, with a focus on the changes observed after the introduction of handwashing procedures in Clinic 1.

## Analysis

The project includes:

- Comparison of mortality between the two clinics;
- Descriptive statistics and data visualization;
- Analysis of mortality before and after handwashing;
- Comparison of mean, median, and mode;
- Hypothesis testing.

## Tools

- tidyverse
